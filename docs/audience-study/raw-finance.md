# Raw simulation: finance, procurement and compliance decision-makers (`finance`)

40 synthetic visitors. CFOs and finance controllers of SMEs, chartered accountants advising MSME clients,
procurement heads running a vendor evaluation, compliance/audit leads in lending, healthcare and education.
25 India, 15 GCC/Singapore (UAE 8, Saudi Arabia 3, Singapore 4). 11 phone, 28 desktop, 1 tablet.
Sceptics (15) are marked **[S]**. Every reaction is based only on what the site shows.

---
