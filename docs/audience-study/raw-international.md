# Audience study: raw notes, segment `international`

40 simulated overseas SME buyers and sector specialists weighing an Indian software partner. Countries: UAE, Saudi
Arabia, Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, UK, Netherlands, Czech Republic, Australia, US. Sectors: healthcare, education,
property/hotels, lending, ecommerce/D2C, logistics, events, non-profit. 14 are written as sceptics (marked **[S]**).
Reactions are based only on what the site says and does (see `brief.md`).

Device split: 18 phone, 21 desktop, 1 tablet.

---

### INT-01 · General Manager, safety and compliance training institute (Dubai, UAE)
60 staff · age 45–54 · tech med · Windows desktop, office fibre, Outlook desktop app · Trigger: trainer scheduling and certificate tracking live in Excel and WhatsApp · Arrived: referral from a peer in the training sector who mentioned "an Indian team that did a big certification system".
- **First 10 s:** Preloader, then the loom. Reads "Custom software for businesses that have outgrown spreadsheets, message threads and manual work." Understands straight away. Stays.
- **Path:** Hero → story → services → skips explorer → Our Work. Stops dead on the Dubai safety training case ("−80% administrative work", "100,000+ certifications a year"). Then How we build → Pricing → contact form.
- **Reaction & opinion:** "That is basically us." The case study matches his sector and his city, which matters more than anything else on the page. Likes "Four stages. No surprises." Finds the 3D pretty but irrelevant. **8/10**
- **Black box:** Is the Dubai case the company his peer meant? No client name, so he will ask the peer. Who will be his contact person?
- **Expected but missing:** A named client or a reference he can call; WhatsApp number.
- **Outcome:** **Contact now.** Form opens Outlook with the message filled in; he sends it. Would have been even faster with a WhatsApp button.
