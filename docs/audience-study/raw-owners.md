# Raw simulation: Indian SME / MSME owners and founders (segment `owners`, 40 people)

Synthetic study. Every reaction is tied to something actually on the site (`src/content/site.ts`, `src/content/pages.ts`,
section order in `src/App.tsx`, contact block in `src/loom/sections.tsx` + `EnquiryForm.tsx`, AI demo in `AIDemo.tsx`).
Mood tag: **S** = sceptic, **H** = in a hurry, blank = neutral/open. 24 on phones, 16 on desktops.

Codes at the end of each block are used for the tallies at the bottom.
Black box: P price · T who the people are · R can't verify clients/results · D data/hosting/security · S support after launch ·
F what happens after the form · O code ownership / exit · L will they come on site / local presence.
Missing: $ price numbers · IC Indian or local client / case · WA WhatsApp button · IT Indian tools (Tally, GST, Zoho, aggregators) ·
FP founder/team page · TA Tamil/regional language · DM demo or real screenshots · TS testimonials/reviews · SC case in my sector ·
CB call button at the top.
