# Keyword and GEO research: Wezigns

Research date: 19 October 2026. Scope: SEO keyword opportunities, competitor SERPs, GEO (being found and cited by
ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews / AI Mode, Gemini, Claude, Copilot) and local SEO for
Wezigns Software Private Limited, Chennai.

**Method and confidence.** No paid keyword tool was available. Demand and difficulty are estimated from live SERPs
(who ranks, how many directories and listicles crowd page one, ad density hints, number of near-duplicate vendor
pages) and from what the AI search summaries surfaced. Read "High / Medium / Low" as relative to each other, not as
volumes. Confidence is noted per cluster. Validate the top 20 terms in Google Search Console (after launch) and the
free Google Keyword Planner before committing content budget.

---

## 0. Findings that matter most (read first)

1. **The site is invisible to AI crawlers today.** Every page ships `<body><div id="root"></div>` and renders in the
   browser. GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and most other AI crawlers fetch HTML but do not run
   JavaScript ([Vercel / MERJ crawler study](https://vercel.com/blog/the-rise-of-the-ai-crawler)). Only Googlebot
   renders, in a delayed second pass. Until copy, headings, links and JSON-LD are in the served HTML (prerender or
   static generation at build time), no GEO work below will pay off. This is the single biggest item.
2. **The brand has almost no entity footprint.** A search for "Wezigns Software Chennai" returned no page about the
   company at all. AI engines cannot cite or recommend an entity they cannot confirm across sources. Directory
   profiles (Clutch, GoodFirms, Justdial, IndiaMART, LinkedIn, Google Business Profile) with identical NAP are the
   fastest fix.
3. **No structured data, sitemap or canonical tags** exist on any page (0 JSON-LD blocks across 14 HTML files;
   `robots.txt` has no `Sitemap:` line).
4. **Copy claims that are hard to cite.** "The leading systems and custom software provider for SMEs and MSMEs" is
   an unverifiable superlative; AI engines favour specific, checkable facts (founded 2012, 11 countries, named
   clients, numbers from case studies). Also: About says "Twelve years, one thread" but 2012 to 2026 is 14 years.
5. **The competitive gap is real.** Page one for core Chennai and India terms is mostly directories (Clutch,
   GoodFirms, IndiaMART), thin vendor pages and "Top 10 companies 2026" listicles. Very few competitors have
   outcome-based pricing explained, SME-specific case studies with numbers, or plain-English FAQs. Wezigns' copy
   already has the raw material for all three.

## 4. GEO checklist (AI answer engines)

### How AI answer engines pick sources (what the evidence says)

- **Google AI Overviews / AI Mode** use the normal index. A page must be indexed and snippet-eligible; Google says
  there are "no additional technical requirements" and no special AI files or schema are needed. Both features use
  "query fan-out" (several related sub-searches), so pages that answer sub-questions clearly get pulled in.
  Best practice listed: allow crawling (including in the CDN), internal links, page experience, important content as
  text, structured data that matches visible text, current Business Profile, Search Console.
  ([Google: AI features and your website](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features))
- **ChatGPT search** depends on **OAI-SearchBot**; blocking it keeps a site out of answers. GPTBot is training only;
  ChatGPT-User fetches pages live for a user and is not a search-inclusion control.
  ([OpenAI: Overview of OpenAI crawlers](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots))
- **Claude**: ClaudeBot (training), Claude-SearchBot (search index), Claude-User (live fetches) are separate robots.txt
  tokens; Anthropic honours robots.txt and Crawl-delay.
  ([Anthropic support: ClaudeBot](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8896518))
- **Copilot** uses Bing's crawl and index; Bing Webmaster Tools added an AI Performance report (citations in Copilot
  and Bing AI summaries) in 2026; IndexNow speeds Bing discovery. No first-party source says IndexNow affects ChatGPT.
  ([eSEOspace summary of Bing guidance](https://eseospace.com/blog/bing-webmaster-tools-indexnow-ai-search/))
- **AI crawlers do not run JavaScript** (Googlebot is the exception).
  ([Vercel: The rise of the AI crawler](https://vercel.com/blog/the-rise-of-the-ai-crawler))
- **Content that gets cited**: the Princeton-led GEO paper (KDD 2024) found adding quotations, statistics and cited
  sources raised visibility up to about 40% (up to 37% on Perplexity); keyword stuffing hurt.
  ([GEO: Generative Engine Optimization, arXiv 2311.09735](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735))
- **Third-party mentions matter more than your own site** for "which company should I use" prompts: a DerivateX study
  of B2B SaaS found ChatGPT cited the recommended vendor's own site only about 12% of the time
  ([DerivateX](https://derivatex.agency/report/chatgpt-software-recommendation-sources/)); listicles and niche sites
  carry most citations ([Overthink Group](https://overthinkgroup.com/b2b-ai-citation-stats-2026-q2/)). Treat these
  agency studies as directional.
- **llms.txt**: low cost, low proven value. Adoption grew from about 4,000 to 36,000 files (June 2025 to May 2026),
  but an Ahrefs analysis found 97% received zero AI requests; Google has said it does not use it; OpenAI has not
  confirmed support. ([PPC Land on the Ahrefs data](https://ppc.land/llms-txt-adoption-rises-8-8x-but-97-of-files-get-zero-ai-requests/),
  [llmstxt.org spec](https://llmstxt.org/)). Publish one because it takes an hour, but do not expect results from it.
- **FAQ rich results** stopped showing in Google on 7 May 2026 for all sites; Google still reads FAQPage markup for
  understanding. ([TechWyse](https://www.techwyse.com/news/ai-search/google-faq-rich-results-deprecated-2026))
  Keep visible Q&A blocks: they are the easiest format for any engine to lift.

## 6. Content gaps worth creating later

Ordered by expected value for both SEO and AI citation.

1. **Case study pages on their own URLs** (`/work/<slug>`): the seller marketplace (15x growth, flat support
   headcount), the event dashboard, the fleet admin, the job portals, the charity platform. Each with problem, system,
   numbers, quote, country, stack. These are the most citable assets Wezigns has.
2. **Pricing guide**: "How much does custom software cost for an SME in India? (2026)". Explain outcome-based pricing,
   what drives cost (modules, integrations, users, process change), indicative ranges by system size, hosting and
   support costs, and how the one-price agreement works. Targets cluster J, which is badly served today.
3. **Build vs buy guides**: "Custom HR system or Keka / greytHR?", "Custom CRM or Zoho / HubSpot?", "When off-the-shelf
   ERP is the better choice". Honest recommendations match the existing FAQ stance and are highly quotable.
4. **System module FAQ blocks** on each `/systems/*` page (5 to 8 real questions each, visible text).
5. **Industry sub-pages** where proof exists: `/systems/industry/healthcare`, `/education`, `/lending`, `/hotels`,
   `/logistics`, `/retail-pos`.
6. **AI use-case pages**: "WhatsApp customer chat for SMEs", "Invoice and document processing with a person in the
   loop", "Back-office agents for approvals". Each with a worked example and what it connects to.
7. **Location pages for international markets** only with local proof: UAE / Dubai, UK, Singapore, Australia,
   USA. Include named clients or case studies there, time-zone overlap, currency and invoicing, contracts.
8. **Software takeover / rescue page**: "Taking over software another company built" (already an FAQ, low
   competition, high-intent).
9. **Integration pages**: Razorpay, Stripe, Shopify, Tally (if offered), WhatsApp Business API, Google Workspace.
10. **Glossary / explainer** pieces for MSME owners (what an ERP is, what MSME Udyam registration means for software
    purchasing, GST e-invoicing basics for billing systems) to earn informational traffic and links.
11. **A yearly "State of SME systems in India" style data post** built from anonymised project data (for example average
    time saved after replacing spreadsheets). Original statistics are the content type AI engines cite most.
